# Hearth & Furrow: launch video plan

- **Angle:** a cozy Tudor farm (c.1530) where nothing can kill your farm. Show the real game doing its thing: grow, craft, village, feasts.
- **Hook:** a storm rolls over the farm: "A storm is coming…" Then a hard cut to sunshine: "Relax. Nothing here can kill your farm."
- **Highlights:** (1) the bread chain (sow, thresh, grind, bake), (2) six villagers on daily schedules, (3) feast days, livestock and village stalls.
- **Punchline/outro:** "Hearth & Furrow · In development · Godot 4.6".
- **Tone:** cozy default: warm and playful, soft wipes, dips through wood-dark, one hard cut (the storm gag).
- **Identity:** UIPalette: parchment #e8d5a8, parchment light #f4e8c8, wood dark #3b2616, wood #6b4423, ink #2a1a0e, gold #f2c14e, moss #5d8a3a, rust #8a3a1c, sky #6f9bb8. Font: PixeloidSans at 36/54/108px (whole multiples of its native 9px). Game captures at an exact 6× (320×180 → 1920×1080), nearest-neighbour.
- **Music:** an original folk loop at 90 BPM in D major: plucked lute voice, recorder melody, frame drum, soft bass, with cut effects in key. 1 beat = 20 frames, 1 bar = 80 frames. 8 bars = 640 frames = 21.333s at 30fps.

## Privacy
Every person on screen is a fictional villager. No save data from a player is used: the capture harness uses a throwaway session.
